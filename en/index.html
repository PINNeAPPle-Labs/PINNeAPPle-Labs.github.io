The problem

Physics explains the world. Artificial intelligence expands what we can do with it.

Simulation helps us understand complex phenomena, but can demand significant time, computing resources, and specialist expertise. Physics AI extends those capabilities when its results can be verified.

FROM PROBLEM TO NUMBER

How an optimization problem becomes a decision

The path is the same with consulting or with Studio: every step leaves an artifact, and verification can reject the result before it becomes a report.

  1. 01

    Problem

    What has to be decided and which number proves the decision

  2. 02

    Geometry and physics

    Domain, materials, equations and boundary conditions

  3. 03

    Reference solver

    The case running in OpenFOAM, FEniCS or CalculiX

  4. 04

    Dataset

    Checked cases with convergence and uncertainty recorded

  5. 05

    Surrogate

    A fast model with its validity limits stated

  6. 06

    Verification and decision

    Comparison against the solver, report and next step

Feedback loop: if verification rejects the result, the case returns to geometry with a new candidate — the number only reaches the report after it passes.

Who makes it

One rule: if it has no measured number, it does not go on screen.

PINNeAPPle started as a personal library so its author would not have to take anything on trust: every result carries the reference it was checked against. It became a product when it was clear the problem is not the model, it is the lack of proof.

Saying “no” is part of it. In one rover-mobility case the classical numerical method was faster and more accurate than the network; the recommendation was the classical method.
