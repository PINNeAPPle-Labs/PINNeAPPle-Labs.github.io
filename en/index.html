Products

Four products, every one with a measured number.

Each exists in code, runs today, and shows its own error. Open the use cases for the external reference behind every figure.

Product 01 · CFD

PINNeAPPle CFD

The full CFD workflow, E1 to E11: you describe the problem, the pipeline builds geometry and mesh, runs OpenFOAM, generates the dataset, trains the surrogate and closes with the evidence map.

  • Erosion in a 90° bend: between 1.05× and 1.49× the published experiment, inside the factor-2 band industry accepts.
  • Surrogate at 1.0% error on pressure drop at points it never saw.
  • 32 simulations from geometry to trained model in 23 minutes.

Product 02 · Surrogates

Surrogate Studio

Turns the simulations your team already ran into a model that answers in microseconds — checked against the solver on every delivery, never against itself.

  • ~200,000× faster than the real 3D simulation, across seven heat-sink geometries.
  • 4.46% mean error and 7.35% worst case against FEniCS/dolfinx.
  • Deterministic prediction: the same numbers on three runs, on different days.
~200,000×faster, at 4.46% error

Product 03 · Verification

VeriPhysics

The gate between the result and the decision: it takes a model, a CFD case or an FEA deck and returns one score per check — residual, convergence, calibration, coverage — with the number and its source.

  • Caught what the loss hid on a client model: residual hundreds of times over the threshold.
  • Unsupported beam: “Job finished” with 1.8e11 mm of displacement; preflight flags FAIL before the run.
  • Mesh: metrics identical to OpenFOAM's checkMesh.

Product 04 · Digital twin

PINNeAPPle Twin

A validated model working next to operational data — and the layer that decides whether it may go on line. Signed contract, preflight, backtest against a redundant probe, and filed evidence.

  • 0.216 °C error on HX-201, with fouling estimated at 0.001651 against a true 0.001637.
  • 95.5% band coverage on the validation window, calibrated on the hours before it.
  • An alarm 2.6 h before the measured crossing, with the forecast error stated.
