pinneapple_client
from pinneapple_client import Client

with Client("https://engineering.pinneapple.org", api_key=KEY) as pea:
    answer = pea.submit("60 W module on a finned aluminium heat sink, air 35 C at 3 m/s")
    job = pea.wait(answer["job_id"])
    result = pea.result(job["id"])
    print(result["evidence"]["status"])   # pass | fail | inconclusive | not_run

GET /v1/lpm/architectures Architectures

What this deployment's LPM endpoint supports, negotiated with the installed library. `available: false means the pinned PINNeAPPle has no LPM module (or no torch behind it): the endpoint then refuses jobs with 501 instead of failing them inside the sandbox. backbones is what the pin's LargePhysicsModel takes, fit what its fit accepts, and design_space_source whether the family is generated by the library's own ellipse_design_space or by the same draws over Design.ellipse`.
