Physics in action

Ten episodes, each with the number and its limit

One video per main area of physics, recorded inside PINNeAPPle Studio: every app is a real service, the button on screen submits the actual solver, and the 3-D field that turns is what the app serves once the job finishes. No frame is a decorative chart.

Each episode has three products: the 60 s technical cut, which explains how the number was measured; the 9:16 short, with the hook; and the raw screen recording. What the clip does not claim sits on each card, beside the number — not in a footnote.
