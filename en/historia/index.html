Story

PINNeAPPle exists because the working had to be visible.

AI models for engineering are easy to sell and hard to believe. PINNeAPPle started as a personal library so its author would not have to take anything on trust: every result carries the reference it was checked against.

Why verification first, and not just a model

Why verification first, and not just a model

The problem

A PINN can hit its target while violating the equation along the way. The training loss looks healthy and the physics residual sits hundreds of times over the threshold — which is exactly what we found in an already-trained model from a real client.

The rule

If it has no number measured against a reference external to training, it does not go on screen. That holds for a README, for a slide and for this landing page.

The cost

Saying "no" is part of the product. In one rover-mobility case the classical numerical method was faster and more accurate than the network — the recommendation was the classical method.

How it is built

How it is built

Open source first

31 modules on PyPI, Apache-2.0, 248 examples with checked output. The base is public because a verification tool nobody can audit verifies nothing.

Public benchmarks

SSQN Helmholtz, LBM vortex shedding, LPM design space, latency and I/O. No internal benchmark presented as proof.

63 applications

Each with a README, numbers and stated limits. They are a starting point for your own product, or proof that the base holds up on the real case.
