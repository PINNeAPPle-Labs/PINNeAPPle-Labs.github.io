Example reports

Seven reports,
seven engineering areas

Every report below is a real study of a case in the repository, in Portuguese and in English. They all follow the same structure: what was asked, the method, the check against an experiment or a second computation route, and the page of what was not proven.

These are public examples, and three of them use demonstration data (machine, structure and climate). What to copy from them is the verification structure, not the number.

Erosion in a 90° bend

Sand in a pipe bend against the Vieira et al. (2016) experiment: erosion rate and where the model stops holding.

Structure

What every report has

  • The scope closed at the start: geometry, fluid, boundary condition and the question.
  • The explicit check: a reference experiment, a second computation route or a gradient check.
  • The number with its uncertainty beside it — never the number alone.
  • A limits page saying what the study does not solve.
  • Provenance: the repository file each number came from.
