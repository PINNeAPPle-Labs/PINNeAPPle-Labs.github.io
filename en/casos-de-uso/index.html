Where we are not there yet: Erosion relies on a factor-2 band, not 1% precision. Flat residuals do not prove drag has settled. And in one cost analysis a classical numerical method was faster and more accurate than the network itself — the recommendation was the classical method. The twin's band is a state band calibrated on a separate window, and its lead comes from a linear extrapolation whose error is reported.
