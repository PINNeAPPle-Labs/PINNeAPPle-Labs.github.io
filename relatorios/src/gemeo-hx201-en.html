TW-201 · HX-201 exchanger twin1 · the request

A shell-and-tube exchanger loses efficiency to fouling. Operations does not want a forecast: it wants to know how much time it has before the downstream load stops closing.

The risk of a twin is not getting the forecast wrong — it is being heard without deserving it. So the study starts with a contract written before any data: the metrics, the ranges and the limits the model must meet in order to exist.

What was contracted

  • Model exchanger-ntu 1.0, with a declared accuracy floor of 1 °C.
  • Acceptance gates: RMSE ≤ 2.5% of span, bias ≤ 1%, coverage ≥ 90%, alarm lead ≥ 1 h.
  • A redundant probe (TT-105) that never enters the filter: it is the judge.
  • Validity range declared in the contract: T from 80 to 110 °C and ṁ from 0.5 to 2.0 kg/s.
Order matters. The contract is signed before the first filter step. A metric chosen after seeing the result is not a metric: it is an excuse.

TW-201 · HX-201 exchanger twin4 · the band, the alarm and what is not proven

The uncertainty band is not a prediction interval: it is a state band, calibrated once on a separate window (the first 127,500 s) and then scored on the rest. The scale applied was ×1.336; without it, the raw ensemble band would cover 86.4% — below the 90% gate.

That is stated in the report, not hidden: a band that only works after being scaled is a scaled band, and the reader needs to know that to decide what to do with it.

The alarm

IndicatorValueReading
Alarm lead9,300 s (2.6 h)time between the warning and the limit crossing
Crossing forecast error3,900 s (1.1 h)distance between the predicted and the measured instant
Where the lead comes fromlinear extrapolation of the latent stateit grows with any change of regime
House rule. Lead time without a stated error is a guess with a clock. The number operations uses is the pair: 2.6 h of warning, with 1.1 h of error on the crossing.

What operations does with it

Lead time is time to plan a cleaning, negotiate a shutdown window or shift load. The twin decides nothing: it warns, and states the error it is warning with.

What this study does not decide

The economic moment of the shutdown, the cleaning vendor and the cost of lost head. It delivers the fouling estimate and the warning window; the rest is an operations decision.
