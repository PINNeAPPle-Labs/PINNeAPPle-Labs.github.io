Os modelos · PINNeAPPle Anchor 1.0.0

A família publicada

Um modelo nomeado por problema de engenharia, publicado com o erro em que foi medido, a faixa em que vale e as checagens que passou — para que a resposta chegue junto com a condição em que ela é verdadeira.

Cada número é o que veio no card do modelo, medido contra a referência que o card nomeia. O domínio e os limites são do card, não desta página: os dois não podem divergir.

4 modelos 4 pesos publicados SHA-256 no manifest

Aerodynamics

Anchor-Aero

Responde. Lift, drag and pitching moment of a 2-D section from its shape and angle of attack.

Substitui. A RANS sweep per design point (meshing + solve) during shape exploration, at Re 4e6, attached to mildly separated flow.

airfoil_cst_rans_mlp v1.0.0 · Veredito APPROVED

Faixa de validade
  • T0 0.1 – 0.24
  • T1 0.08 – 0.22
  • T2 0.08 – 0.22
  • M0 -0.03 – 0.08
  • M1 -0.02 – 0.14
  • M2 -0.04 – 0.12
  • alpha_deg -2 – 14
  • reynolds: 4e+06
  • mach: incompressible
  • geometry_family: 2nd-order Bernstein CST, upper = M + T, lower = M - T, trailing-edge thickness 0.25 % chord
  • regime: fully turbulent (k-omega SST), attached to mildly separated; deep stall (Cl swing > 0.1 over the last 500 iterations) was excluded from training
Não vale para
  • Drag at high lift reads ~14 % high on the campaign grid (20k cells) compared with finer grids/XFOIL; the surrogate inherits the solver's bias, it does not correct it.
  • Not valid in deep stall, at other Reynolds numbers, compressible flow or outside the CST bounds.
  • Accuracy is against the same solver and grid that generated the training data (interpolation error); the solver's own error against experiment is the larger term.

Terramechanics

Anchor-Contact

Responde. Wheel-soil contact forces of a rigid grousered wheel on lunar regolith simulant.

Substitui. A discrete-element wheel-soil simulation per slip and load condition during mobility studies.

Erro medido (held-out)

test/Fx_rmse_N · 0.22test/Fz_rmse_N · 0.47test/My_rmse_Nm · 0.0707

rover_contact_grc1_viper v1.1.0 · Veredito APPROVED

Faixa de validade
  • slip -0.4 – 0.9
  • sinkage_m 0.002 – 0.06
  • soil: GRC-1 (pinneapple terramechanics soil database)
  • wheel: VIPER wheel, R 0.25 m, b 0.20 m, 24 grousers
  • slip_angle_deg: 0
  • physics: Wong-Reece with slip-sinkage n = n0 + n1 s on driving slip (GRC-1: n0 0.8, n1 0.8); steady state, so the slip that sets the exponent equals the slip
Não vale para
  • Only as good as the Wong-Reece model: against published single-wheel tests the model's drawbar pull RMSE is 4-14 N (digitised data), larger than the surrogate's own error.
  • One soil and one wheel; zero slip angle (no lateral force); steady state.
  • Slip-sinkage makes the forces kink at zero slip (the exponent grows only with driving slip); the network smooths the kink over a small slip interval.

Thermal

Anchor-Thermal-Forced

Responde. Base-to-air thermal resistance and pressure drop of a plate-fin heat sink in ducted forced convection.

Substitui. A conjugate-heat-transfer solve per fin geometry during heat-sink sizing.

  • Ducted flow: real heat sinks with bypass lose 20-50 % of the airflow; correct the velocity first.
  • Convection correlations carry a typical +-15 % accuracy; the surrogate's own error (<1 % mean) is against the engine, not against a measurement.
  • Screening tool: re-verify a chosen design with the physics engine.

Thermal

Anchor-Thermal-Natural

Responde. Base-to-air thermal resistance of a plate-fin heat sink in natural convection.

Substitui. A buoyancy-driven CFD solve per fin geometry where no fan is available.

Erro medido (held-out)

heatsink_natural_mlp v1.0.0 · Veredito APPROVED

Faixa de validade
  • base_width_mm 30 – 200
  • base_depth_mm 30 – 200
  • base_thickness_mm 2 – 15
  • fin_thickness_mm 0.6 – 4
  • fin_height_mm 8 – 80
  • k_w_mk 150 – 400
  • source_frac_w 0.15 – 1
  • source_frac_d 0.15 – 1
  • t_ambient_c 0 – 50
  • fin_gap_mm 2.2 – 30.6
  • power_w 1 – 80
  • fin_gap_note: gap sampled in 2.5-20 mm, then the fin count is rounded; the realised gaps of the training designs span 2.2-30.6 mm
  • regime: natural convection in free air, vertical fins, no radiation
Não vale para
  • Natural convection without radiation: at low power, radiation can carry a comparable share of the heat for black-anodised sinks; the reference does not model it.
  • Correlation-based reference (typical +-15 % against experiments); the surrogate's own error is against the engine, not against a measurement.
  • The engine's resistance rises with power on 0.5 % of designs (non-monotone correlation blending); the power law is checked on the model but not enforced in training.
  • Screening tool: re-verify a chosen design with the physics engine.
